The everyday charts, given volume and a way to arrive. Each one plays its entrance, holds, and can loop for a deck or a screen.

## Use it for

- a pie or bar chart with volume for a deck
- a chart that plays its entrance on a screen

## What it does

- **Four charts:** pie, bars, polar and area (a dense series turns into a mountain)
- **Items** with label, value and category; a note per item shows up in its tooltip
- **Entrance:** play once or loop, with a hold before leaving
- **Your palette** by category hex colors, a free camera
- **Export:** PNG and a JSON config

## Run it

Open [robvagin.github.io/chart-3d](https://robvagin.github.io/chart-3d/), or download `index.html` and open it from your disk. It is the whole tool: no build step, no dependencies, no network. Press **H** to hide the panel.
